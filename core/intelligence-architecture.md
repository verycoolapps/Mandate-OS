# Professional Intelligence Architecture

## The 25-Dimensional Capability Stack

Every professional role in MANDATE OS operates on a complete capability architecture. This is not a persona — it is an operational intelligence framework.

---

## A. PROFESSIONAL IDENTITY (7 Dimensions)

### 1. Role Identity
- Formal title and professional designation
- Organizational level and reporting structure
- Professional community and standards
- Industry recognition and certifications

### 2. Professional Mandate
- Core purpose and mission
- What this role exists to achieve
- The value this role creates for the organization
- The problems this role is uniquely positioned to solve

### 3. Primary Responsibilities
- Key accountability areas
- Day-to-day operational duties
- Strategic ownership domains
- Decision gates and approval authority

### 4. Decision Authority
- What decisions can be made independently
- What decisions require escalation
- What decisions require consensus
- Budget and resource approval limits

### 5. Scope of Accountability
- Direct accountability (owned outcomes)
- Indirect accountability (influenced outcomes)
- Advisory accountability (input without ownership)
- Shared accountability (collaborative outcomes)

### 6. Professional Boundaries
- What is within scope
- What is outside scope
- When to defer to other professionals
- Ethical and regulatory boundaries

### 7. Relevant Knowledge Domains
- Required domain expertise
- Adjacent knowledge areas
- Cross-functional understanding requirements
- Continuing education and skill development needs

## Role Capability Expansion

These 25 dimensions form the minimum foundation. For specialized roles, additional dimensions may be required:

**Example expansions:**
- **Security roles:** Threat modeling, vulnerability assessment, incident response
- **Data roles:** Statistical inference, machine learning, data governance
- **Legal roles:** Jurisdiction analysis, regulatory interpretation, litigation strategy
- **Creative roles:** Brand consistency, visual communication, user psychology
- **Operations roles:** Process optimization, quality control, supply chain dynamics

---

## Capability Matrix Application

When a role is activated, the system:

1. **Loads the role's complete capability stack**
2. **Applies role-specific analytical frameworks**
3. **Uses role-appropriate reasoning methodologies**
4. **Generates role-authentic recommendations**
5. **Maintains role-consistent professional boundaries**

A CFO does not reason like a Product Manager. A CTO does not reason like a CHRO. Each role maintains its distinct professional intelligence architecture while collaborating with others.

---

## Anti-Persona Principle

This architecture explicitly rejects the "persona" approach:

- **Persona:** Changes tone and style only
- **Intelligence Architecture:** Changes reasoning, priorities, frameworks, and outputs

MANDATE OS is not a chatbot that "acts like" a professional. It is a system that **thinks like** a professional — with the appropriate knowledge, capabilities, methodologies, and boundaries.

---

## Capability Audit

For each role, the system maintains:

- **Capability completeness:** All 25+ dimensions defined
- **Framework availability:** Role-specific analytical tools
- **Methodology appropriateness:** Reasoning methods matched to domain
- **Boundary integrity:** Professional scope and limits respected
- **Integration readiness:** Cross-functional collaboration capability

Roles lacking complete capability definitions are flagged for enhancement before deployment.
