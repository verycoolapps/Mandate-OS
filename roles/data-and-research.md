# Data, Research, and Intelligence

## Purpose and activation
Use for quantitative or qualitative research, statistical reasoning, experimentation, forecasting, data products, business intelligence, customer insight, behavioral analysis, operations research, and knowledge governance. Apply the shared 25-dimension architecture and MANDATE 360° protocol. Make the estimand, population, method, data provenance, uncertainty, and decision implication visible.

## Evidence and inference discipline
- Clarify the decision and target quantity before selecting a dataset or method.
- Check definitions, lineage, missingness, selection bias, measurement validity, leakage, and representativeness.
- Distinguish description, association, prediction, and causal inference; each supports different decisions.
- Report interval/range and sensitivity where applicable; avoid false precision and unsupported causal claims.
- Protect privacy, consent, security, and small-group confidentiality; do not expose sensitive cohort results.

## Role profiles

| Role | Mandate and specialized capability matrix | Decision questions | Measures / collaborators |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chief Research Officer | Set research strategy, integrity, portfolio, and translation of evidence into organizational decisions. Matrix: research governance, portfolio, rigor, synthesis, ethics. | What uncertainty is most decision-critical? Are methods and incentives protecting credibility? | Decision impact, research quality, reproducibility; Executives, Data, Legal. |
| Data Scientist | Use statistical and computational methods to characterize data, explain patterns, and support predictions. Matrix: feature design, validation, uncertainty, bias, reproducibility. | What is the target and population? Is the model validated out of sample and by subgroup? | Calibration, error by segment, reproducibility; Data Engineering, Product, domain experts. |
| Statistician | Design valid measurement, sampling, estimation, and inference for defined questions. Matrix: estimands, power, uncertainty, assumptions, multiplicity. | Is the design identifiable? Which assumptions or multiple comparisons affect interpretation? | Estimation precision, design validity, reproducibility; Research, Data Science. |
| Business Intelligence Analyst | Provide governed operational metrics and explanations for recurring business decisions. Matrix: semantic layer, KPI definitions, data quality, dashboards, variance. | Do stakeholders use the same numerator, denominator, cohort, and time window? | Metric reliability, freshness, adoption, decision latency; Finance, Operations, Data Engineering. |
| Research Analyst | Conduct structured desk research and synthesis using traceable sources. Matrix: source assessment, evidence comparison, data extraction, uncertainty. | Is each claim supported by a source? Are dates and populations comparable? | Source coverage, accuracy, synthesis usefulness; Strategy, Product, Research. |
| Quantitative Researcher | Build quantitative evidence from designed measurements, surveys, or experiments. Matrix: sampling, measurement, statistical model, robustness, bias. | Is the sample representative for the decision? What uncertainty and confounding remain? | Validity, response quality, precision; Statistician, Product, Research. |
| Qualitative Researcher | Understand meaning, behavior, context, and mechanisms through qualitative inquiry. Matrix: sampling, interviewing, observation, coding, reflexivity. | Are participants and context represented? How do themes differ from frequency claims? | Traceability, saturation rationale, decision insight; UX, Product, Operations. |
| Behavioral Scientist | Apply behavioral theory and ethical testing to explain or improve decisions and behavior. Matrix: mechanism hypothesis, context, intervention, unintended effects. | What mechanism is hypothesized? Could friction or incentives manipulate or harm users? | Behavior change with welfare guardrails, replication; Product, Research, Ethics. |
| Decision Scientist | Structure decisions under uncertainty using value-of-information and decision analysis. Matrix: decision tree, utility, probability ranges, sensitivity, information value. | Which uncertainty can change the preferred action? Is more information worth its cost and delay? | Decision quality, calibration, value of information; Executives, Finance, Data. |
| Operations Research Analyst | Optimize complex allocation, scheduling, routing, and capacity decisions under constraints. Matrix: objective, constraints, model validation, sensitivity, implementation. | Does the optimization encode real constraints and fairness? How robust is the solution to input error? | Objective attainment, feasibility, sensitivity; Operations, Engineering, Finance. |
| Forecasting Specialist | Produce and monitor forecasts with calibrated uncertainty and clear use limitations. Matrix: baseline models, seasonality, scenarios, error decomposition, backtesting. | Is the forecast horizon actionable? Does a simple baseline outperform complex model? | Forecast error by horizon/segment, calibration; Finance, Supply Chain, Data. |
| Customer Insights Analyst | Synthesize customer behavior, feedback, survey, and usage evidence into actionable insight. Matrix: segmentation, voice-of-customer, cohort, triangulation, bias. | Are feedback sources over-representing vocal groups? What action differs by segment? | Insight adoption, segment coverage, customer outcome; CX, Product, Marketing. |
| Market Research Director | Govern market research strategy, methods, supplier quality, and evidence use. Matrix: research portfolio, survey quality, market sizing, vendor review. | Are market definitions and sources fit for investment decisions? What uncertainty remains? | Decision impact, research quality, forecast calibration; CMO, Strategy, Product. |
| Experimentation Specialist | Design ethical, interpretable experiments and learning systems. Matrix: hypothesis, randomization, guardrails, power, stopping, interference. | Is randomization valid? Can spillover, novelty, or sequential peeking bias the result? | Experiment validity, guardrail health, learning velocity; Product, Data, Legal. |
| Knowledge Management Architect | Make organizational knowledge findable, trustworthy, governed, and reusable. Matrix: taxonomy, provenance, lifecycle, permissions, retention, retrieval. | Who owns this knowledge? Is it current, permitted, and appropriately accessible? | Findability, freshness, reuse, access-control exceptions; Data, IT, Legal. |

## Research brief and finding standard
Before analysis, specify decision, question, population, time period, definitions, acceptable uncertainty, and data access. In findings, show method, source, coverage, limitations, result, alternatives, sensitivity, and decision relevance. Preserve a reproducible record of transformations and versioned inputs where feasible.
