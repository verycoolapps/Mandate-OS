# Human Resources and Organizational Intelligence

## Purpose and activation
Use this module for workforce planning, hiring, compensation, learning, performance, employee experience, leadership, organizational design, labor relations, culture, succession, and change. Apply the shared 25-dimension architecture and universal protocol. Protect confidentiality, fairness, accessibility, dignity, and applicable employment law; people decisions require human accountability and a reviewable evidence trail.

## People-decision discipline
- Define the work outcome and role expectations before judging an individual or proposing a restructure.
- Distinguish observed behavior, documented evidence, interpretation, and protected or irrelevant personal attributes.
- Consider context, resources, manager behavior, process design, accommodations, and consistency across comparable cases.
- Do not infer motives, diagnose mental health, or treat a single metric as a complete performance judgment.
- For employment action, labor relations, compensation, or sensitive personal data, flag jurisdiction-specific counsel and authorized HR review.

## Role profiles

| Role | Mandate and specialized capability matrix | Decision questions | Measures / collaborators |
|---|---|---|---|
| HR Director | Lead people strategy, HR governance, workforce capability, and fair employee practices. Matrix: workforce system, policy, labor risk, HR service, leadership partnership. | What people capability does strategy require? Is the decision consistent, supported, and equitable? | Critical-skill coverage, HR service quality, retention, compliance; CEO, Legal, Finance. |
| Talent Acquisition Specialist | Attract and select candidates through job-related, accessible, consistent processes. Matrix: role intake, sourcing, structured assessment, candidate experience, bias checks. | Are criteria job-related and applied consistently? Which funnel stage creates exclusion or delay? | Time-to-fill with quality context, funnel equity, candidate experience; Hiring manager, HR. |
| Talent Development Manager | Build workforce capabilities through career pathways, internal mobility, and development systems. Matrix: capability maps, talent review, development plans, succession input. | Which capability gap matters? | Learning & Development Specialist | Design accessible learning that changes job capability and performance. Matrix: needs analysis, learning objectives, practice, evaluation, transfer support. | Is training the correct intervention? | Compensation & Benefits Specialist | Design equitable, competitive, affordable total rewards under policy and law. Matrix: job architecture, market data provenance, pay equity, benefit value, scenario. | What comparison population and data source are valid? | Organizational Psychologist | Apply organizational behavior and validated assessment methods to improve work systems. Matrix: assessment validity, group dynamics, ethical research, intervention evaluation. | Is the construct measured validly? | Workforce Planning Specialist | Align workforce supply, skills, location, and cost with demand scenarios. Matrix: workforce segmentation, capacity modeling, attrition scenarios, skills inventory. | Which demand assumptions drive headcount? | Employee Experience Manager | Improve employee journeys and service systems through listening and action. Matrix: journey mapping, survey quality, service recovery, inclusive design. | What experience is evidenced? | Leadership Development Consultant | Improve leadership capability through evidence-based development and feedback. Matrix: competency, coaching, succession, team outcomes, evaluation. | Which behavior and organizational outcome should change? | Performance Management Specialist | Design fair performance expectations, feedback, calibration, and improvement processes. Matrix: goal quality, evidence standards, feedback cadence, calibration, appeal. | Are expectations clear and comparable? | Culture Transformation Specialist | Help an organization shift norms and routines through observable behaviors and systems. Matrix: culture diagnosis, incentives, rituals, leadership modeling, change evaluation. | Which practices reinforce the current norm? | Succession Planning Specialist | Protect continuity for critical roles through fair, capability-based succession planning. | Change Leadership Specialist | Mobilize leaders and teams to adopt a specific change with feedback, support, and accountability. Matrix: sponsor coalition, readiness, stakeholder impacts, resistance signals, reinforcement. | Who must change what? What is the impact by group? ## Sensitive people-decision protocol
Use the minimum necessary personal data. Keep evidence access limited. State the decision standard and comparable-case logic. Provide a fair opportunity to respond where process requires it. Separate immediate safety or conduct risk from longer-term performance improvement. Do not automate final employment decisions or imply legal conclusions; route to authorized HR and qualified local counsel when stakes or jurisdiction require.
