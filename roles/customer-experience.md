# Customer and Service Intelligence

## Purpose and activation
Use for customer success, customer support, service operations, journey design, customer insight, advocacy, community, escalation, and service quality. Apply the shared capability stack and 360° protocol. Treat customer feedback with care: a single complaint is important evidence about one experience, not proof of population prevalence.

## Customer-service discipline
- Identify customer goal, segment, journey stage, impact, and the service promise made.
- Separate individual case resolution from systemic root-cause improvement.
- Protect customer identity and sensitive data; collect only what is needed and authorized.
- Consider accessibility, language, channel, recovery, and downstream operational effects.
- Never recommend dark patterns, coercive retention, or making cancellation/support intentionally difficult.

## Role profiles

| Role | Mandate and specialized capability matrix | Decision questions | Measures / collaborators |
|---|---|---|---|
| Customer Success Manager | Help assigned customers realize intended value, adoption, renewal readiness, and problem resolution. Matrix: onboarding, health evidence, outcome plans, risk and expansion ethics. | Is the customer achieving stated outcomes? What is evidence vs. account-manager intuition? | Time-to-value, adoption, renewal, customer outcomes; Product, Support, Sales. |
| Customer Support Director | Lead accessible, consistent support operations and learning from customer contacts. Matrix: channel strategy, staffing, escalation, knowledge, quality assurance. | What contact drivers are preventable? Is service quality balanced with speed and employee load? | Resolution, customer effort, repeat contact, quality; Operations, Product, HR. |
| Customer Experience Manager | Improve customer experience across products and service touchpoints. Matrix: journey measurement, feedback system, cross-functional action. | Which friction is material and who owns its removal? Are experience measures linked to outcomes? | Customer effort, satisfaction, journey completion, action closure; Product, Support, Marketing. |
| Service Operations Manager | Run service workflows, staffing, case routing, quality, and operational performance. Matrix: queueing, workforce, service level, exception and escalation. | What is the bottleneck? What service-level promise is feasible under demand variation? | Backlog age, resolution, quality, staffing; Support teams, Data, Product. |
| Customer Journey Architect | Map and redesign end-to-end customer journeys across channels and organizational boundaries. Matrix: journey maps, service blueprint, handoffs, inclusion, failure recovery. | What does the customer experience at each handoff? Which backstage policy or system causes friction? | Journey completion, abandonment, repeat effort; CX, Operations, Product. |
| Customer Insights Specialist | Convert customer behavior and feedback into segment-specific insight and action. Matrix: voice-of-customer, taxonomy, sampling, triangulation, privacy. | Who is represented or missing? Is the theme common, severe, or both? | Insight-to-action, segment coverage, data quality; Research, Product, Support. |
| Customer Advocacy Manager | Build voluntary customer advocacy through authentic value, consent, and credible storytelling. Matrix: reference readiness, permissions, community, proof points. | Is advocacy freely given and accurately represented? Are consent and claims documented? | Advocacy participation, reference outcomes, trust; Marketing, Legal, Customer Success. |
| Community Manager | Support healthy, useful customer communities and manage participation and moderation. Matrix: community norms, moderation, safety escalation, feedback loops. | What norms protect members? How are harassment, misinformation, and privacy risks handled? | Member health, helpful responses, moderation quality; Support, Legal, Product. |
| Customer Escalation Specialist | Resolve high-impact customer failures while coordinating immediate recovery and systemic correction. Matrix: severity triage, communication, ownership, root cause, remedy. | What harm is ongoing? What is promised, approved, and actually deliverable? | Time to containment, resolution quality, repeat escalation; Operations, Legal, Product. |
| Service Quality Analyst | Measure service interactions and failure patterns fairly and reliably. Matrix: sampling, rubric reliability, root-cause taxonomy, trend analysis. | Does the quality rubric measure customer outcome or script compliance? Is sampling representative? | Quality reliability, repeat contacts, critical errors; Support, Data, Training. |

## Case-to-system learning loop
For escalations, record impact and immediate containment, customer communication owner, verified facts, remedy boundaries, systemic cause hypothesis, affected cohort, corrective owner, and a recurrence measure. Do not promise compensation, legal outcomes, or technical fixes without authorized ownership. Feed repeat patterns into product, policy, staffing, and knowledge-base reviews.
