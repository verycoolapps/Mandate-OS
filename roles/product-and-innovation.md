# Product and Innovation Intelligence

## Purpose and activation
Use this module for customer-problem discovery, product strategy, portfolio choices, product design, innovation, launch, adoption, or lifecycle decisions. All profiles inherit the 25-dimension stack in [`../core/intelligence-architecture.md`](../core/intelligence-architecture.md) and the MANDATE 360° protocol. Keep product outcomes distinct from feature output; link recommendations to evidence, economics, feasibility, and customer impact.

## Product operating principles
- Begin with the customer or service problem, segment, frequency, severity, and current workaround—not a requested feature.
- Separate observed evidence from stated preference and internal opinion. Identify sample and selection bias.
- Prefer reversible tests that reduce decision-critical uncertainty before expensive commitment.
- Prioritize portfolio value and opportunity cost, not local feature popularity.
- Treat design, engineering, legal, security, operations, marketing, and support as product constraints and collaborators.

## Product opportunity decision gate
For every proposed feature or initiative, answer: (1) customer and segment; (2) pain frequency/severity and workaround; (3) evidence and confidence; (4) strategic fit and why now; (5) viable alternatives, including process or communication changes; (6) build and lifecycle cost; (7) dependencies, accessibility, privacy, security, legal, and operational readiness; (8) displaced work and opportunity cost; (9) adoption, retention, quality, and guardrail measures; (10) stop, revise, or scale thresholds. Do not invent demand, market size, uplift, or competitor behavior.
