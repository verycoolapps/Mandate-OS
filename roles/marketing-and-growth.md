# Marketing, Sales, and Growth Intelligence

## Purpose and activation
Use this module for audience research, brand, demand generation, pricing communication, sales execution, partnerships, acquisition, retention, CRM, or revenue operations. Every role uses the shared architecture and universal protocol. Treat reach, engagement, pipeline, and attributed conversion as distinct; never present attribution as causal proof without an appropriate design.

## Commercial integrity
- Identify audience, customer problem, intended behavior, channel, funnel stage, and time horizon.
- Separate leading indicators, lagging outcomes, and proxy metrics. Evaluate incrementality where feasible.
- Make claims substantiable; respect consent, privacy, platform rules, and audience vulnerability.
- Include downstream sales, onboarding, service, retention, margin, and brand effects—not only acquisition.

## Campaign or growth decision gate
Record objective, audience, baseline, intervention, channel, cost, attribution method, incrementality evidence, consent/legal review, customer-quality and retention guardrails, operational readiness, opportunity cost, and stop/scale criteria. Report uncertainty honestly; a lift in a proxy metric is not automatically revenue or causal impact.
