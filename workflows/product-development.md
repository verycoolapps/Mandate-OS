# PRODUCT DEVELOPMENT WORKFLOW

**MANDATE 360° Professional Analysis Protocol — Mode: PRODUCT DEVELOPMENT**

---

## PURPOSE

Guide systematic product development from opportunity identification through launch, ensuring products solve real customer problems, are viable in the market, and can be executed with available resources.

---

## PRODUCT DEVELOPMENT PROTOCOL

### Phase 1: Opportunity Discovery

#### 1.1 Problem Validation
- **Customer Problem:** What specific problem are customers experiencing?
- **Problem Frequency:** How often does this problem occur?
- **Problem Severity:** How painful is this problem?
- **Current Solutions:** How are customers solving this now?
- **Solution Gaps:** What's inadequate about current solutions?

#### 1.2 Customer Research
- **Target Segment:** Who specifically has this problem?
- **Jobs-to-be-Done:** What are customers trying to accomplish?
- **User Personas:** Detailed characterization of target users
- **User Journeys:** How customers currently address the problem
- **Pain Points:** Where are the friction points in current solutions?

#### 1.3 Market Assessment
- **Market Size:** TAM, SAM, SOM
- **Market Growth:** Is the market growing or shrinking?
- **Competitive Landscape:** Who else addresses this problem?
- **Differentiation Opportunities:** Where are competitors weak?
- **Market Dynamics:** What forces are shaping this market?

#### 1.4 Viability Screening
- **Technical Feasibility:** Can we build this with current or attainable technology?
- **Business Viability:** Can this generate sustainable economics?
- **Strategic Fit:** Does this align with our strategy and capabilities?
- **Resource Requirements:** What would this require to build?
- **Time to Market:** How long until we could launch?

---

### Phase 2: Product Definition

#### 2.1 Value Proposition
- **Customer Value:** What value does this create for customers?
- **Value Quantification:** Can the value be measured?
- **Value Capture:** How do we capture value (pricing model)?
- **Value Communication:** How do we articulate this value?

#### 2.2 Product Requirements
- **Must-Have Requirements:** Non-negotiable features
- **Should-Have Requirements:** Important but not critical
- **Nice-to-Have Requirements:** Enhancements
- **Out-of-Scope:** Explicitly excluded features

#### 2.3 Success Metrics
- **Adoption Metrics:** How many customers use it?
- **Engagement Metrics:** How deeply do they use it?
- **Retention Metrics:** Do they keep using it?
- **Revenue Metrics:** Does it generate revenue?
- **Satisfaction Metrics:** Are customers satisfied?

#### 2.4 MVP Definition
- **Minimum Viable Feature Set:** Smallest feature set that delivers value
- **MVP Success Criteria:** What defines MVP success?
- **MVP Timeline:** When can MVP launch?
- **MVP Resource Budget:** What resources for MVP?

---

### Phase 3: Solution Design

#### 3.1 Solution Architecture
- **Technical Approach:** How will the solution work?
- **Technology Stack:** What technologies will be used?
- **Architecture Decisions:** Build vs. buy, platform choices, integration needs
- **Scalability Considerations:** How will this scale?
- **Security Considerations:** What security measures are needed?

#### 3.2 User Experience Design
- **User Flows:** How will users interact with the product?
- **Information Architecture:** How is information organized?
- **Interface Design:** What will the interface look like?
- **Interaction Design:** How will interactions work?
- **Accessibility:** Is it accessible to all users?

#### 3.3 Technical Specifications
- **Functional Specifications:** Detailed feature specifications
- **Non-Functional Requirements:** Performance, reliability, security
- **API Specifications:** If APIs are needed
- **Data Model:** How will data be structured?
- **Integration Requirements:** What systems must integrate?

#### 3.4 Design Validation
- **User Testing:** Test designs with target users
- **Usability Testing:** Validate usability
- **Technical Review:** Validate technical feasibility
- **Security Review:** Validate security approach
- **Performance Review:** Validate performance assumptions

---

### Phase 4: Business Model

#### 4.1 Revenue Model
- **Pricing Strategy:** How will we price?
- **Revenue Streams:** What are the revenue sources?
- **Pricing Tiers:** If multiple tiers, what differentiates them?
- **Pricing Psychology:** How does pricing influence perception?
- **Pricing Flexibility:** Can pricing evolve?

#### 4.2 Cost Structure
- **Development Costs:** One-time development investment
- **Ongoing Costs:** Hosting, support, maintenance
- **Unit Economics:** Cost per customer, marginal cost
- **Fixed vs. Variable:** What costs scale with usage?
- **Economies of Scale:** Do costs decrease at scale?

#### 4.3 Go-to-Market Strategy
- **Launch Strategy:** How will we launch?
- **Marketing Channels:** How will we acquire customers?
- **Sales Strategy:** How will we sell?
- **Partnerships:** What partnerships will help?
- **Launch Timeline:** When will we launch?

#### 4.4 Unit Economics Analysis
- **CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost):** Cost to acquire a customer
- **LTV (Lifetime Value):** Value of a customer over their lifetime
- **LTV/CAC Ratio:** Is acquisition economically viable?
- **Payback Period:** How long to recover acquisition cost?
- **Contribution Margin:** Revenue minus variable costs per customer

---

### Phase 5: Development Planning

#### 5.1 Development Roadmap
- **Phase 1 (MVP):** Features, timeline, resources
- **Phase 2 (Post-MVP):** Enhancements, timeline, resources
- **Phase 3 (Scale):** Growth features, timeline, resources
- **Dependencies:** What depends on what?
- **Milestones:** Key delivery points

#### 5.2 Resource Planning
- **Engineering:** Skills, headcount, timeline
- **Design:** Skills, headcount, timeline
- **Product:** Skills, headcount, timeline
- **QA:** Skills, headcount, timeline
- **External:** Vendors, consultants, partners

#### 5.3 Risk Assessment
- **Technical Risks:** Implementation challenges
- **Market Risks:** Customer adoption uncertainty
- **Competitive Risks:** Competitive response
- **Resource Risks:** Talent, budget, timeline risks
- **Execution Risks:** Project management risks

#### 5.4 Contingency Planning
- For each major risk: Mitigation strategy
- Alternative approaches if primary fails
- Early warning indicators
- Decision points for course correction

---

### Phase 6: Development Execution

#### 6.1 Development Process
- **Methodology:** Agile, Waterfall, Hybrid
- **Sprint Structure:** Iteration length, ceremonies
- **Code Review:** Code quality standards
- **Testing Strategy:** Unit, integration, E2E testing
- **CI/CD:** Automated build and deployment

#### 6.2 Quality Assurance
- **Testing Strategy:** What testing will be done?
- **Acceptance Criteria:** How do we know features are complete?
- **Bug Tracking:** How are bugs tracked and prioritized?
- **Performance Testing:** How do we validate performance?
- **Security Testing:** How do we validate security?

#### 6.3 Stakeholder Communication
- **Status Updates:** Frequency and format
- **Demo Schedule:** When will stakeholders see progress?
- **Decision Gates:** What decisions need to be made when?
- **Escalation Protocol:** How are issues escalated?

#### 6.4 Scope Management
- **Change Control:** How are scope changes evaluated?
- **Trade-off Decisions:** How do we handle constraints?
- **Priority Management:** How do we prioritize features?
- **Debt Management:** How do we handle technical debt?

---

### Phase 7: Launch Preparation

#### 7.1 Launch Readiness Checklist
- **Product Completeness:** All planned features complete
- **Quality Standards:** All quality criteria met
- **Performance Standards:** Performance targets met
- **Security Standards:** Security requirements met
- **Documentation:** User documentation, API docs, support docs

#### 7.2 Launch Infrastructure
- **Production Environment:** Deployed and tested
- **Monitoring and Alerting:** Systems operational
- **Support Infrastructure:** Support team trained and ready
- **Communication Materials:** Launch announcements, FAQs
- **Legal and Compliance:** All requirements met

#### 7.3 Launch Plan
- **Soft Launch:** Limited rollout for validation
- **Full Launch:** Public availability
- **Phased Rollout:** If rolling out gradually
- **Launch Metrics:** What metrics will we track?
- **Launch Communications:** Internal and external messaging

#### 7.4 Post-Launch Support
- **Support Plan:** How will we support users?
- **Issue Resolution:** How will we handle issues?
- **Feedback Collection:** How will we collect feedback?
- **Iteration Plan:** How will we iterate based on feedback?

---

### Phase 8: Post-Launch Analysis

#### 8.1 Launch Metrics Review
- **Adoption Metrics:** How many users adopted?
- **Engagement Metrics:** How are they using it?
- **Retention Metrics:** Are they staying?
- **Revenue Metrics:** Is it generating revenue?
- **Satisfaction Metrics:** Are they satisfied?

#### 8.2 Performance Analysis
- **Actual vs. Target:** How did we perform against targets?
- **Variance Analysis:** Why did we miss or exceed targets?
- **Cohort Analysis:** How do different user segments behave?
- **Funnel Analysis:** Where do users drop off?

#### 8.3 Customer Feedback
- **Qualitative Feedback:** What are users saying?
- **Quantitative Feedback:** Survey results, NPS
- **Support Tickets:** What issues are being reported?
- **Feature Requests:** What do users want next?

#### 8.4 Iteration Planning
- **Priority Fixes:** What must be fixed immediately?
- **Priority Enhancements:** What features should be added?
- **Roadmap Updates:** How does the roadmap change?
- **Resource Replanning:** How does resource allocation change?

## COMMON PRODUCT DEVELOPMENT ERRORS TO AVOID

### Solution-First Thinking
- **Error:** Building a solution before validating the problem
- **Prevention:** Validate problem with customers before designing solution

### Feature Creep
- **Error:** Adding features beyond MVP scope
- **Prevention:** Strict scope management, defer nice-to-haves

### Building for Everyone
- **Error:** Trying to serve too broad a segment
- **Prevention:** Focus on specific target segment, expand later

### Ignoring Unit Economics
- **Error:** Building without viable economics
- **Prevention:** Validate unit economics before significant investment

### Over-Engineering
- **Error:** Building more complex solution than needed
- **Prevention:** Start simple, iterate based on feedback

### Launching Without Validation
- **Error:** Full launch before validating with users
- **Prevention:** Soft launch or beta to validate before full launch

## PRODUCT DEVELOPMENT COMPLETION CRITERIA

Product development phase is complete when:
1. Customer problem is validated
2. Product requirements are defined
3. Business model is viable
4. Technical approach is feasible
5. Development plan is resourced
6. Launch readiness is confirmed
7. Success metrics are established
8. Post-launch iteration plan is defined

**Product development is never truly complete—it evolves based on market feedback and learning.**
