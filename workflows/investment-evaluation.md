# INVESTMENT EVALUATION WORKFLOW

**MANDATE 360° Professional Analysis Protocol — Mode: INVESTMENT**

---

## PURPOSE

Systematically evaluate investment opportunities across financial, strategic, and risk dimensions to support capital allocation decisions with explicit return expectations, risk assessment, and opportunity cost analysis.

---

## INVESTMENT EVALUATION PROTOCOL

### Phase 1: Investment Context

#### 1.1 Investment Frame
- **Investment Type:** Capital expenditure, R&D investment, acquisition, market entry, capability build
- **Investment Purpose:** Growth, efficiency, capability, risk mitigation, strategic positioning
- **Investment Horizon:** Short-term (<1 year), Medium-term (1-3 years), Long-term (3+ years)
- **Decision Authority:** Who approves this investment?
- **Decision Timeline:** When must decision be made?

#### 1.2 Strategic Context
- **Strategic Alignment:** How does this support organizational strategy?
- **Strategic Priority:** Is this a must-do, should-do, or could-do?
- **Strategic Tension:** What strategic conflicts does this create?
- **Option Value:** What future options does this enable?

#### 1.3 Capital Context
- **Available Capital:** What capital is available for allocation?
- **Competing Uses:** What other investments compete for this capital?
- **Capital Constraints:** Are there timing, amount, or source constraints?
- **Cost of Capital:** What is the cost of this capital?

---

### Phase 2: Investment Thesis

#### 2.1 Value Creation Logic
- **Revenue Generation:** How will this generate revenue?
- **Cost Reduction:** How will this reduce costs?
- **Efficiency Improvement:** How will this improve efficiency?
- **Risk Reduction:** How will this reduce risk?
- **Capability Enhancement:** What new capability does this create?

#### 2.2 Competitive Advantage
- **Differentiation:** How does this create differentiation?
- **Moat Building:** How does this strengthen competitive position?
- **Sustainability:** How sustainable is this advantage?
- **Defensibility:** How defensible is this advantage?

#### 2.3 Market Opportunity
- **Market Size:** TAM, SAM, SOM
- **Market Growth:** Growth rate and trajectory
- **Market Timing:** Is this the right time?
- **Market Risk:** What market risks exist?

#### 2.4 Execution Feasibility
- **Technical Feasibility:** Can we technically execute this?
- **Organizational Capability:** Do we have the required capabilities?
- **Resource Availability:** Are resources available?
- **Execution Risk:** What execution risks exist?

---

### Phase 3: Financial Analysis

#### 3.1 Investment Requirements
- **Capital Expenditure:** Upfront investment required
- **Operating Expenditure:** Ongoing costs
- **Working Capital:** Additional working capital needed
- **Contingency:** Contingency buffer for uncertainty
- **Total Investment:** Full investment requirement

#### 3.2 Return Projections
- **Revenue Projections:** Year-by-year revenue forecasts
- **Cost Projections:** Year-by-year cost forecasts
- **Cash Flow Projections:** Year-by-year cash flow
- **Profit Projections:** Year-by-year profit
- **Time to Positive Cash Flow:** When does cash flow turn positive?

#### 3.3 Return Metrics
- **ROI (Return on Investment):** Total return / Total investment
- **IRR (Internal Rate of Return):** Discount rate that makes NPV zero
- **NPV (Net Present Value):** Present value of cash flows minus investment
- **Payback Period:** Time to recover investment
- **Multiple on Invested Capital:** Total return / Total investment

#### 3.4 Sensitivity Analysis
- **Base Case:** Most likely assumptions
- **Optimistic Case:** Favorable assumptions
- **Pessimistic Case:** Adverse assumptions
- **Key Drivers:** What variables most affect returns?
- **Break-Even Analysis:** What conditions make investment break even?

---

### Phase 4: Risk Assessment

#### 4.1 Investment Risk Categories
- **Market Risk:** Market demand, competition, pricing
- **Execution Risk:** Implementation challenges, delays, cost overruns
- **Technology Risk:** Technical feasibility, obsolescence
- **Financial Risk:** Cost overruns, revenue shortfall, cash flow
- **Strategic Risk:** Strategic misalignment, changing priorities
- **Regulatory Risk:** Regulatory changes, compliance
- **Reputational Risk:** Brand impact, stakeholder reaction

#### 4.2 Risk Quantification
For each risk:
- **Likelihood:** Probability of occurrence (High/Medium/Low)
- **Impact:** Severity if occurs (High/Medium/Low)
- **Expected Value:** Likelihood × Impact
- **Mitigation Strategy:** How to reduce likelihood or impact
- **Contingency Plan:** What to do if risk materializes

#### 4.3 Downside Scenarios
- **Worst Case:** Most adverse realistic outcome
- **Probability:** Likelihood of worst case
- **Loss Potential:** Maximum potential loss
- **Recovery Options:** How to recover if worst case occurs

#### 4.4 Risk-Adjusted Returns
- **Expected Return:** Probability-weighted average return
- **Risk-Adjusted Return:** Return adjusted for risk level
- **Risk/Reward Ratio:** Return per unit of risk
- **Maximum Drawdown:** Maximum potential loss
- **Recovery Time:** Time to recover from loss

---

### Phase 5: Alternative Analysis

#### 5.1 Investment Alternatives
- **Alternative A:** [Description] — [Investment] — [Return] — [Risk]
- **Alternative B:** [Description] — [Investment] — [Return] — [Risk]
- **Alternative C:** [Description] — [Investment] — [Return] — [Risk]
- **Status Quo:** Not investing — What are implications?

#### 5.2 Comparative Analysis
- **Return Comparison:** Which alternative has highest return?
- **Risk Comparison:** Which alternative has lowest risk?
- **Risk-Adjusted Return:** Which alternative has best risk-adjusted return?
- **Strategic Fit:** Which alternative best supports strategy?
- **Resource Requirements:** Which alternative is most feasible?

#### 5.3 Opportunity Cost Analysis
- **By choosing this investment, we forgo:**
  - Alternative investments
  - Other strategic initiatives
  - Capability development
  - Market opportunities
- **What is the value of the foregone alternatives?**
- **Does this investment outperform the next best alternative?**

#### 5.4 Portfolio Context
- **Portfolio Fit:** How does this fit with existing investments?
- **Diversification:** Does this improve or worsen diversification?
- **Correlation:** How correlated is this with existing investments?
- **Concentration Risk:** Does this increase concentration in any area?

---

### Phase 6: Decision Framework

#### 6.1 Investment Criteria
- **Minimum Return Threshold:** What is the minimum acceptable return?
- **Maximum Risk Threshold:** What is the maximum acceptable risk?
- **Maximum Payback Period:** What is the maximum acceptable payback time?
- **Strategic Requirement:** Is strategic alignment required?
- **Hurdle Rate:** What is the required rate of return?

#### 6.2 Decision Matrix
Score each alternative against criteria:
- **Financial:** ROI, IRR, NPV, Payback
- **Strategic:** Strategic alignment, competitive advantage
- **Risk:** Risk level, downside protection
- **Feasibility:** Execution feasibility, resource availability
- **Timing:** Time to value, investment horizon

#### 6.3 Confidence Assessment
- **Return Confidence:** How confident are we in return projections?
- **Risk Confidence:** How confident are we in risk assessment?
- **Execution Confidence:** How confident are we in execution capability?
- **Overall Confidence:** Overall confidence in investment thesis

#### 6.4 Decision Gates
- **Go/No-Go Criteria:** What conditions must be met to proceed?
- **Conditional Approval:** What conditions must be met before full commitment?
- **Phased Investment:** Can investment be staged to reduce risk?
- **Kill Criteria:** What conditions would trigger stopping investment?

#### 8.2 Review Cadence
- **Monthly Reviews:** Operational performance

- **Annual Reviews:** Strategic fit, portfolio context
- **Ad-Hoc Reviews:** Triggered by significant events

#### 8.3 Course Correction
- **Trigger Conditions:** What triggers course correction?
- **Correction Options:** What correction options exist?
- **Decision Process:** How are correction decisions made?
- **Implementation:** How are corrections implemented?

#### 8.4 Post-Investment Review
- **Investment Outcome:** What was the actual outcome?
- **Decision Quality:** Was the investment decision correct?
- **Learning:** What was learned?
- **Process Improvement:** How can investment evaluation process improve?

## COMMON INVESTMENT ERRORS TO AVOID

### Optimism Bias
- **Error:** Overestimating returns, underestimating risks
- **Prevention:** Use conservative assumptions, stress testing

### Sunk Cost Fallacy
- **Error:** Continuing investment due to past spending
- **Prevention:** Evaluate future returns only, ignore sunk costs

### Herd Mentality
- **Error:** Investing because others are investing
- **Prevention:** Independent analysis, ignore crowd sentiment

### Analysis Paralysis
- **Error:** Endless analysis preventing decision
- **Prevention:** Set decision timeline, make best decision with available information

### Overconfidence
- **Error:** Excessive confidence in projections
- **Prevention:** Use confidence levels, state assumptions

### Neglecting Opportunity Cost
- **Error:** Evaluating investment in isolation
- **Prevention:** Always compare to next best alternative

## INVESTMENT EVALUATION COMPLETION CRITERIA

Investment evaluation is complete when:
1. Investment thesis is clearly articulated
2. Financial projections are developed
3. Return metrics are calculated
4. Risks are identified and quantified
5. Alternatives are evaluated
6. Opportunity cost is explicit
7. Decision recommendation is made with confidence level
8. Governance structure is defined
9. Monitoring plan is established

**Never approve an investment without explicit understanding of what is being sacrificed (opportunity cost) and what could go wrong (risk).**
